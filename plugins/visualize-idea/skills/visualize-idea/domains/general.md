# Domain pack: general

Used when no other pack fits (an idea, a policy, a story, a set of notes). Drawing hints only: draw what the text says; this pack is not a checklist.

## Recognition
Any text that does not clearly belong to one of the other packs.

## Terms and writing style
- Keep the text's own words; do not translate them into technical terms.
- Additions follow the text's tone: an informal note stays informal.

## Diagram choice
- Steps or a procedure → flowchart.
- Things and how they relate (concepts, parts, people) → flowchart used as a graph, without start/end or decisions.
- Situations and what changes them → state diagram.
- Who does what → swimlane.
- Figures → XY chart.
- When the text mixes several of these, propose the one that matches its main question, and mention the others.

## How concepts are drawn
- The main idea or subject → the first box, top or left.
- Groups of related points → subgraphs with the text's heading as title.
- Open questions the text itself raises → boxes with role suggestion (❓), traced to the question in the text.

## Role hints
Use few roles: start and end for a clear beginning and end, external for outside parties, inactive for "maybe later".
