# Domain pack: planning

Project plans, requirements and use cases. Drawing hints only: draw what the text says; this pack is not a checklist.

## Recognition
Words like project, phase, milestone, task, deliverable, dependency, deadline, resource, effort, budget, requirement, use case, actor, scope, release.

## Terms and writing style
- Task, milestone and requirement names and numbers exactly as written (`REQ-12`, "M2 Prototype").
- Dates and effort as written; missing ones become `[?]` open points.

## Diagram choice
- Tasks and their dependencies (what must be done before what) → flowchart, left to right.
- Who does which tasks → swimlane (one lane per person or team).
- Budget, effort or progress figures the text gives → XY chart.
- Use cases with their actors → flowchart with the actors as boxes (role external) and use cases as rounded boxes `UC1("Pay invoice")`; the steps of one use case → flowchart or sequence.
- Phases and the conditions to move to the next one → state diagram.
- Dates on a time line (Gantt) are a later candidate type; until then, put dates in the labels.

## How concepts are drawn
- Milestones → stadium shapes with role start (project start) or end (final delivery); other milestones without role.
- Dependencies → lines from the earlier task to the later one, no labels needed.
- Out-of-scope or later items → role inactive.
- Risks the text names → role warning.

## Role hints
start / end (project start, final delivery), warning (risks), inactive (later phases, out of scope), external (suppliers, customers, other teams).
