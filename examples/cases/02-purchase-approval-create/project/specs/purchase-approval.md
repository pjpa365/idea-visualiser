# Purchase approval procedure

Any employee can request a purchase. The employee fills in a purchase request with the item, the supplier and the amount, and sends it to their team lead.

The team lead reviews the request. If the team lead rejects it, the employee is informed and the procedure ends. If the team lead approves it and the amount is below 5,000 euro, the request goes straight to Purchasing. Requests of 5,000 euro or more also need the approval of Finance.

Finance checks the request against the department budget. When the budget is not sufficient, Finance rejects the request and the employee is informed. Otherwise Finance approves it and passes it to Purchasing.

Purchasing places the order with the supplier and sends the order confirmation to the employee. When the goods arrive, the employee confirms receipt in the system, after which Purchasing closes the request.

If Finance has not handled a request within 5 working days, the request is escalated to the finance manager.
